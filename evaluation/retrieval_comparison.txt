UniGuide AI retrieval comparison
================================

Method
------
The same six questions, model, and verified index were used for both runs.
The index contains 26 PDFs, 290 chunks, and 290 normalized 384-dimensional
vectors. The model ran on CPU from the local cache. Each run returned three
primary passages. The revised run could attach at most one continuation
outside those three. All reported scores are unadjusted cosine similarities.
The repository copy records rankings and evidence summaries. Consult the
original PDFs through data/sources.csv for passage text.

Expected policy in top three: 6/6 before, 6/6 after.
Expected policy at rank one: 5/6 before, 6/6 after.
This comparison measures document retrieval and manually checked evidence on
six questions. It supplies no corpus-wide accuracy estimate.

1. What are the rules for registering for courses at AIU?
---------------------------------------------------------
Before: Enrollment #3 (0.7391), Registration #1 (0.7074),
        Enrollment #2 (0.6929).
After:  Registration #1 (0.7074), Enrollment #3 (0.7391),
        Registration #7 (0.6302).
        Continuation for #1: Registration #2 (0.6357).

Before, the direct policy supplied the study-plan and adviser rules and the
usual load. Enrollment cross-referenced the limits and occupied two slots.
After, #2 adds the credit limits and summer exception. #7 adds the calendar,
forms, review, payment, and financial-hold requirements.

Remaining gap: Concrete calendar dates require the academic calendar.
Detailed late-registration, add/drop, and withdrawal rules remain outside this
result set.

2. What medical services are available to students at AIU?
---------------------------------------------------------
Before: Medical #5 (0.6537), Medical #6 (0.5977), Disability #16 (0.5881).
After:  Medical #5 (0.6537), Medical #2 (0.5849), Disability #16 (0.5881).
        Continuation for #2: Medical #3 (0.4438).

Before, the medical passages focused on ambulance transport, staff dependents,
and employees' pre-placement examinations. After, #2 adds primary care,
on-campus emergency response, walk-in access, and the start of the medication
rules. #3 supplies the rest of the prescription exclusions and refill rules.

Remaining gap: The top hit still contains staff-dependent material, and the
third hit concerns disability. This evidence gives a clinic overview; clinic
hours and a detailed list of services for students are absent from these hits.

3. How can a student submit an appeal or complaint at AIU?
--------------------------------------------------------
Before: Appeals #2 (0.7559), Appeals #1 (0.6869), Disability #30 (0.6806).
After:  Appeals #2 (0.7559), Appeals #5 (0.6727), Disability #30 (0.6806).

Before, #2 gave the format and content of a written submission, while #1
repeated the general scope and protections. After, #5 provides the recipient,
referral, recommendation, and approval or decline process. Together #2 and #5
support the general submission procedure.

Remaining gap: The final-grade appeal deadline, charges, and response timeline
are on page 2 in other indexed passages. Those details were outside both top
threes.

6. What are the tuition and fee payment requirements for students at AIU?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before: Tuition #1 (0.7545), Tuition #2 (0.7076), Admission Transfer #2 (0.6760).
After:  Tuition #1 (0.7545), Tuition #4 (0.6532), Tuition #2 (0.7076).

Before, #2 covered due dates, currency, and semester versus credit-hour
billing. After, #4 adds charge categories, good financial standing, and
consequences of nonpayment, while #2 is still present.

Remaining gap: The deferred-payment terms on page 2 are outside both result
sets. The student can apply for two installments under the conditions in the
policy. The retrieved passages also omit current tuition amounts.
